# Popdarts Shopify theme

Before writing or changing any section, block, snippet or template, follow the rules in @SHOPIFY_RULES.md.
Before delivering, run `node tools/shopify-check.js` on every file you deliver and on its export/Downloads copies.
Don't hand anything over until it reports 0 errors. Length limits: section, block and preset names are 25
characters max, and select/radio option labels are 50 max. Other schema text must stay within the ceilings
listed in SHOPIFY_RULES.md section 1.
